- **`.safetensors` files uploaded to Mlmodel repositories now expose their tensor structure as artifact metadata** (#4402, #2382). After the upload commits, the server reads only the 8-byte header length and the JSON header (capped at 8 MiB; at most two parses run at once) through ranged storage reads, never the tensor data, and records each tensor's dtype and shape, the total and per-dtype parameter counts, and the header's `__metadata__` string map under `safetensors` in `artifact_metadata` (format `mlmodel`). The stored document stays under 1 MiB: the per-tensor listing and `__metadata__` are cut to size budgets and flagged `tensors_truncated` / `metadata_truncated`. It is returned in the `metadata` field of `GET /api/v1/repositories/{key}/artifacts/{path}` and `GET /api/v1/artifacts/{id}`, and by `GET /api/v1/artifacts/{id}/metadata`. A malformed or unreadable header never fails the upload; a short reason is recorded as `safetensors_error` instead. Applies to generic `PUT`, multipart and chunked uploads; GGUF and ONNX are not parsed yet.
