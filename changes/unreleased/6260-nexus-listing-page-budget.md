- **A Nexus listing call now fetches at most 50 upstream pages, so a pause or cancel takes effect within seconds during a date-windowed migration. `MIGRATION_NEXUS_LIST_PAGE_BUDGET` changes the limit** (#6260). Nexus has no server-side date filter, so a narrow `date_from`/`date_to` window over a large repository could walk the whole repository in one call, and the pause and cancel checks could not run until it finished. A call now returns what it has when it reaches the limit, even nothing, and the job carries on from the same place without fetching any page twice. A larger value means fewer, longer calls; `0` or an invalid value keeps 50. The pre-migration assessment of a Nexus repository walks up to 200 component pages and, for raw and Maven repositories, up to 400 asset pages. Its count is exact for at most 2,000 components and, for raw and Maven, at most 4,000 assets in the asset listing. Otherwise it is marked not exact: normally a lower bound, though past the 1,000,000-path dedupe cap it can be too high. Both date bounds are exclusive on Artifactory and Nexus sources, so chained incremental runs should overlap their windows (see `docs/migration-date-window.md`).
