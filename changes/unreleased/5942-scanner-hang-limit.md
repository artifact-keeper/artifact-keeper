- **A scanner that never returns no longer leaves its scan `running` for half an hour** (#5942). A queued scan job (`SCAN_QUEUE_ENABLED`) that ran out of its 600 s budget was dropped whole: the hung scanner's `scan_results` row (and on an on-demand scan the rows of the scanners after it) stayed `running` until the stuck-scan janitor failed them after `STUCK_SCAN_THRESHOLD_SECS`. Each scanner in a queued job now stops when the budget runs out, its row is recorded `failed`, and the job is retried. The scanners of one job share the budget, so two hung scanners no longer use it up one after the other. The new `SCANNER_PROCESS_TIMEOUT_SECS` (unset by default, so nothing that finishes today is cut short) limits each local `grype` or `trivy` process in queued and in-process scans: past it the process is killed and its row recorded `failed`, and a queued job is retried. Scans through the scanner adapter keep the adapter's own budgets. This replaces the 1.12.2 attempt (#6100), whose fixed 300 s limit cut off slow scans that had been finishing and never retried them.
