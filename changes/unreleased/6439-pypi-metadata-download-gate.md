- **PyPI PEP 658 metadata is refused when its distribution is refused or held** (#6439). `.metadata` was extracted from a distribution's `artifacts` row with no download gate, so for a row that was quarantined, rejected or refused by the repository's scan policy the wheel answered `409`/`403` while its METADATA answered `200`, directly and through a virtual. The row now passes the same download gate as the distribution before anything is read from it: a hold answers `409`, a rejection or a refusing scan policy `403`, and through a virtual the refusal ends the member walk. Two cases followed the member's own policy rather than the one the wheel is served under. In a virtual whose combined scan policy scans (#3023) over a Remote member that does not, a cached wheel's row was read for its METADATA while the wheel itself went through the scan gate (`403`). The row is now left to the member's Remote path, as for the wheel. On that path, and on a Remote's own route, the metadata follows the cached wheel the way a conda `.sigs` sidecar follows its package (#4585): a cached wheel inside its Package Age Policy hold answers `409`, and while the policy scans, a stored verdict for the wheel's digest that blocks under the severity gate answers the wheel's `scan_blocked` `403`. Under `fail_closed`, a cached wheel with no verdict yet is decided by the wheel's own scan gate on the cached bytes, so the metadata gets the wheel's `423` or `403`. The gate fails closed: an unreadable cache sidecar, a cached wheel with no recorded digest, or a verdict lookup that fails answers `503` with `Retry-After`, never the metadata. With no cached wheel the metadata is served and the wheel download stays gated. The metadata also passes the download age gate the wheel passes (`451` for a withheld version), and a Remote's own route gates a row it holds for the distribution, as its file route does. Status-code change: `.metadata` requests that answered `200` in these cases now answer `409`, `403`, `423`, `451` or `503`.
