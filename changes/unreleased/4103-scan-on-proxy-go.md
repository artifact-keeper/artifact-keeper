- **Scan-on-proxy now gates Go module zips from Go Remote and Virtual repositories** (#4103, part of #4097). With `scan_on_proxy` on, a proxied `@v/{version}.zip` goes through the shared inline scan-and-block gate instead of streaming unscanned: a vulnerable zip is refused with 403 `scan_blocked`, a clean one is served with `X-AK-Scan: clean`, the verdict is reused for the same digest, and `fail_open`, `fail_closed` and `record_only` behave as they do for the other enforced formats. The zip must be the module the request names (every entry under `{module}@{version}/`, and a `go.mod` that declares that module); one that is not is withheld under `fail_closed` (423) without being scanned. A zip that agrees is graded as the requested `module@version` (through a synthetic `go.mod` pin, #4952) and on its contents, chiefly the requirements in its own `go.mod` (see "Archive formats graded on their contents" in `docs/security/scan-on-proxy.md`). A Virtual repository applies the stricter of its own and each Remote member's policy, and a member's block or fail-closed lock is final. On Remote and Virtual Go repositories (whether or not scan-on-proxy is on), a module path or version containing `?`, `#`, `;`, `\`, a stray `%`, a space or a control character after decoding is now refused with `400` on every route: a `?` in the version let the `.mod` and `.info` routes fetch the module zip from upstream and serve it unscanned. `.mod`, `.info`, `@v/list` and `@latest` are otherwise unchanged, and `GET /api/v1/formats` now reports Go as `scan_on_proxy: "enforced"`.
