- **Stored artifacts can be rescanned after a vulnerability-database update, at the lowest queue priority** (#5947). A verdict is only as fresh as the database it was graded against, and until now a stored artifact kept its old result after a Grype or Trivy database update until someone rescanned it by hand. With the durable scan queue on, `SCAN_RESCAN_MAX_PER_DAY` (default `0`, off) lets the scan worker queue rescans when a scanner reports a newer database build than the one an artifact's latest scan used: Grype's database as `grype db status` reports it, or the build a scanner recorded with a recent scan, as the scanner adapter does for Trivy. Each content digest is one `rescan` job at a new band below hosted scans, so interactive and hosted scans always overtake a rescan backlog, and the digest is scanned once for every artifact row in the instance that holds it: the first row gets a fresh result and the others reuse it. Most recently downloaded artifacts go first, at most `SCAN_RESCAN_MAX_PER_DAY` digests a day, once per database build. A rescan adds results and never removes or rewrites the earlier ones. `ak_scan_queue_depth{kind="rescan"}` and `ak_scan_queue_oldest_pending_seconds{kind="rescan"}` report the pending rescans and the oldest one's age (migration 384). Left at `0`, nothing is selected or queued, so upgrading does not start any rescans.
