- **With `SCAN_WORKER_CONCURRENCY` unset, the scan worker now runs no more jobs at once than `MAX_CONCURRENT_SCAN_EXTRACTIONS` allows** (#6033). The worker fell back to a fixed 4 whatever `MAX_CONCURRENT_SCAN_EXTRACTIONS` was set to, so a pod with `MAX_CONCURRENT_SCAN_EXTRACTIONS=1` claimed four jobs, three of which held their claim and lease while they waited on the one extraction permit, and the `running` count in `GET /api/v1/admin/security/scan-queue` and the `ak_scan_worker_slots_*` metrics overstated the work in progress. Unset now means 4 or the extraction limit, whichever is lower, so lowering `MAX_CONCURRENT_SCAN_EXTRACTIONS` lowers the claims but raising it does not raise them; a set `SCAN_WORKER_CONCURRENCY` is used as before. A worker whose extraction limit is 1 now runs one job at a time, and while a rescan holds it (rescans take at most half the slots, one on a single-slot worker) other jobs wait; set `SCAN_WORKER_CONCURRENCY` to 2 or more to keep a slot free.
