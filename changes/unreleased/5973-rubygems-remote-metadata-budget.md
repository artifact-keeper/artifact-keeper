- **A burst of RubyGems Remote `/versions` requests can no longer stall every format's metadata** (#5973). Each Compact Index, specs index or quick gemspec fetch on a RubyGems Remote reserved the full 128 MiB cap from the shared 1 GiB buffered-metadata budget and waited for it without a bound, so eight concurrent cold or slow `/versions` fetches held the whole budget and npm, Maven, PyPI and every other format's metadata requests queued behind them. RubyGems Remote documents now draw on a sub-budget of their own, a quarter of the shared budget by default (`AK_RUBYGEMS_REMOTE_METADATA_BUDGET_BYTES` overrides it), and a request that cannot get its share within 10 seconds answers 503 with `Retry-After`. A fresh cached document reserves its own size rather than the cap. With both the VS Code gallery and RubyGems shares full, the headroom always left to every other format drops from 640 MiB to 384 MiB (three 128 MiB buffers). The RubyGems share is a fraction of `AK_PROXY_METADATA_BUDGET_BYTES`, so where that was lowered the share is proportionally smaller (never below one 128 MiB document); `AK_RUBYGEMS_REMOTE_METADATA_BUDGET_BYTES` raises it. A single request is served as before, and no configuration change is needed.
