- **The backend image's bundled `grype` moves to upstream v0.121.3 and its bundled `buildctl` is rebuilt with `golang.org/x/net` v0.62.0, clearing every HIGH and CRITICAL finding in the 1.11.12 image** (#5565). A scan of 1.11.12 found no HIGH or CRITICAL issues in the UBI OS packages. Every one it reported came from the two Go binaries the image builds from source. In `grype`, Grype reported GO-2026-6888 (CRITICAL, `github.com/andybalholm/brotli` v1.2.0, fixed in 1.2.7), GO-2026-6865 (HIGH, `github.com/klauspost/compress` v1.20.1, fixed in 1.20.2) and GO-2026-4887 (HIGH, `github.com/docker/docker` v28.5.2, no fix), and Trivy reported CVE-2026-41567 and CVE-2026-42306 against the same `docker/docker` module. In `buildctl`, Trivy reported CVE-2026-78661 (HIGH, `golang.org/x/net` v0.60.0, fixed in v0.62.0). grype v0.121.3 (commit `cd1f23eb`) pins brotli v1.2.8, klauspost/compress v1.20.3, `x/net` v0.62.0 and `go-jose/v4` v4.1.6 itself. It also no longer depends on `github.com/docker/docker`: its engine client moved to `github.com/moby/moby/client`. So the `x/net` and `go-jose` overrides in `docker/Dockerfile.backend{,.alpine}` have reached their removal conditions and are deleted, and the `x/crypto` floor stays (v0.121.3 ships v0.58.0). The five `github.com/docker/docker` tokens in `.trivyignore` (CVE-2026-33997, -34040, -41567, -41568, -42306) are tombstoned. The module is gone from every scanner binary main ships, so `.trivyignore` now has no live suppressions. No BuildKit release pins `x/net` v0.62.0 yet (v0.34.2 still pins v0.59.0), so `buildctl` stays on v0.33.1 and only its existing `x/net` override's target moves from v0.60.0 to v0.62.0. `grype --version` now prints `grype 0.121.3` and `grype version` reports `Supported DB Schema: 6` on go1.27.3, so the pre-seeded v6 database and the scanner-version parsing are unchanged. `buildctl --version` still reports v0.33.1.
