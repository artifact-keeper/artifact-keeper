- **A download served through a virtual repository by one of its Remote members is now counted** (#4535, #3844). Both virtual-download resolvers recorded only a hosted (Local/Staging) member that won, so a virtual download answered by a Remote member (a presigned redirect, a proxy-cache hit or an upstream fetch) was missing from both `download_statistics` and `proxy_download_statistics`, and an all-proxy deployment fronted by a virtual repository reported no downloads at all. The winning Remote member is now recorded once in `proxy_download_statistics` under the member's own repository and path, the same row a direct download from that Remote writes. Members that were probed but lost to a higher-priority member are not counted, and a `HEAD` is never counted. Applies to every format whose Virtual download goes through the shared resolvers; the formats with their own member walk (NuGet, PyPI, Helm) already recorded their Remote serves and are unchanged.
