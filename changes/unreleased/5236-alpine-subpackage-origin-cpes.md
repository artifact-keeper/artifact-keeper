- **An Alpine subpackage such as `libcurl` is now graded against its origin package's NVD advisories** (#5236). Scan-on-proxy grades a `.apk` through a synthetic installed-package record that names the package's `origin`. The engine matched the distribution feed through that origin, but its NVD (CPE) search for the origin only rewrites the subpackage's own generated CPE (`libcurl:libcurl` becomes `curl:curl`), which misses products NVD files under another vendor (`haxx:curl`). `libcurl 8.14.1-r4` graded clean with 0 findings, and passed a `fail_closed` gate, while `curl` from the same source got 40. A survey of Alpine 3.22 `main` found 30 such subpackages (`libcurl`, six `python3` subpackages, `dnsmasq`, `apache2` and others). The record now also names the origin package itself at the same version, so the engine searches the CPEs it knows for the origin. What it reports for that record is attributed to the package being served: findings name the subpackage, an advisory matched both ways counts once, and the origin is not added to the package's SBOM or to the component count its verdict records. A subpackage now carries its origin's advisories, including ones that affect only another part of the origin (a `curl` command-line CVE on `libcurl`, or the origin's advisories on a `-doc` subpackage). This over-reports in the safe direction.
