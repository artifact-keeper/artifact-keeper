- **A multipart publish whose envelope exceeds `MAX_UPLOAD_SIZE` is now refused with `413 Payload Too Large`, not `400`** (#4023). The JetBrains plugin upload and the Swift publish parse their `multipart/form-data` body with `multer` under a `whole_stream` ceiling equal to `max_upload_size_bytes`. That ceiling always trips before the staging primitive's own per-part byte count can -- it measures the envelope, the stager measures one part -- and `multer`'s size-limit error was reported as a "malformed multipart" `400`: straight from the parser when a small body arrived in one piece, or through the stager's read-failure mapping when a chunked body crossed the ceiling mid-part. A raw (non-multipart) upload of the same size answers `413`, which is also what both handlers' comments promised. `multer`'s size-limit error is now mapped to `413` by a shared helper on both surfaces, including when it surfaces inside `stage_stream_content_addressed`; regression tests cover both delivery shapes. Follow-up to the #3848 fix in #3942, which also gains a byte-level pin of the raw `X-Plugin-Name` upload path and a database-free binary-fixture check of the multipart parser.
