- **A first install through a scanning Remote no longer loses scans to the queue** (#5231). The 30-second budget for an inline proxy scan started when the scan began waiting for a permit, not when it began running. Under `fail_closed`, a file could answer `423 scan_pending` with its scan barely started, which failed `npm install` on a host with little CPU to spare. Under `record_only` and `fail_open`, everything still queued 30 seconds after an install's burst of requests was dropped: in an `express` install, about half of the 67 files got no verdict on 24 cores, and 53 of them with two CPUs. An over-budget scan was killed too, so a retry started from nothing.

  A file scan now runs in its own task and requests wait on it. The 30 seconds count from when the scan starts, and a `fail_closed` request waits at most 60 seconds for that (`AK_PROXY_SCAN_QUEUE_WAIT_SECS`). Either way it is answered within three quarters of `GLOBAL_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECS` of arriving. A request that stops waiting is still refused, and `fail_closed` still serves no byte without a verdict, but a running scan goes on for up to 120 seconds, and a retry joins it or finds its verdict. `record_only` and `fail_open` scans stay queued until they run; a queued `fail_closed` scan leaves the queue 30 seconds after the last request waiting on it has gone. A verdict recorded while a scan ran is no longer overwritten by it, and a verdict records the scanner version the scan started with.

  The limit of four concurrent scans is unchanged. New limits bound what waits: 4,096 scans in flight and 1,024 per repository, 1 GiB of held bytes and half of that per repository (`AK_PROXY_SCAN_QUEUE_BUDGET_BYTES`), and 256 waiting requests per repository and 16 per scan. A file past one of them gets no scan for now, and counts in the new `ak_proxy_scan_queue_refused_total`. Behind a proxy with a 60-second read timeout, as nginx has by default, a long wait now answers `504` rather than `423`: see "Timeouts in front of the gate" in `docs/security/scan-on-proxy.md`. Image manifest scans are unchanged.
