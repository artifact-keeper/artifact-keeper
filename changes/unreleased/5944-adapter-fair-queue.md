- **The scanner adapter can share its scan slots fairly between clients, run background scans at a lower priority, and export queue metrics** (#5944). With `SCANNER_CLIENT_LIMITS_FILE` (JSON or YAML, re-read on change) each client, identified by its registry host (or, for a filesystem upload, the `X-Scan-Client` header or remote address), gets a `max_concurrent` cap and a `weight`, and a freed slot goes to the next client by weighted deficit round robin instead of global arrival order, so one busy Artifact Keeper instance or registry can no longer hold every other client's scans behind its backlog. A scan request may carry `"priority": "background"` (`X-Scan-Priority` for filesystem uploads): such a scan runs only while no interactive scan is waiting, or after `SCANNER_BACKGROUND_MAX_WAIT` (default 30m). `SCANNER_METRICS_ADDR` serves `scanner_adapter_jobs_queued{priority}`, `scanner_adapter_jobs_running`, `scanner_adapter_slots_capacity`, `scanner_adapter_queue_wait_seconds` and `scanner_adapter_jobs_total{outcome}` on a separate listener, with per-client series behind `SCANNER_METRICS_CLIENT_LABEL`. All of it is opt-in: without these settings, scans are scheduled exactly as before.
