- **With the durable scan queue on, hosted upload and pushed-image scans are now claimed ahead of a fail-open proxy scan backlog, and that backlog ages so it still drains** (#6032). Hosted scans sat in the lowest band below every `fail_open` and `record_only` proxy scan, with no aging, so a burst of cold proxy fetches held back every upload and push verdict until the proxy backlog was empty: about 480 queued proxy scans made upload scans wait a median of about 17.6 minutes. The claim order is now a `fail_closed` pull's job first, then hosted scans and proxy backfill merged by age, with each backfill job counted as queued `SCAN_QUEUE_BACKFILL_AGING_SECS` (default `600`; `0` for plain age order) later than it was, then rescans. A hosted scan goes ahead of any younger backfill, and a backfill job goes ahead of a hosted scan queued more than the window after it, so neither can starve the other. Hosted jobs queued by 1.12.0 (at the old priority 10) are claimed as hosted too, including those a 1.12.0 pod queues during a rolling upgrade. The window bounds the head start, not an upload's wait behind a backlog older than the window. The claim still reads each band as a range of the existing pending-claim index; no migration.
