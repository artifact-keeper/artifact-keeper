- **A virtual conda channel whose member cannot be fetched or parsed now fails with 502 naming the member instead of serving a smaller index** (#4555, #4192). The merge logged the failure at `warn` and served the members that did answer with 200, so a client saw a valid-looking `repodata.json` with an entire member missing, concluded those packages did not exist, or resolved a name present in two members to the lower-priority one. Conda virtual `repodata.json` (every encoding) and `channeldata.json` now answer `502 Bad Gateway` with a body naming each failed member and the cause (fetch error, size ceiling, decode or parse error). An operator can opt a virtual repository into degraded merges with `PATCH /api/v1/repositories/{key}` `{"virtual_metadata_partial": true}`: the merge of the healthy members is then served with `X-AK-Partial-Members: <member keys>`, `Cache-Control: no-store` and a `Warning: 199`. Every member failure increments `ak_virtual_member_metadata_failures_total{format,virtual_repo,member,reason}` under either policy. Members the caller may not read are not failures. The shared `collect_virtual_metadata` helper used by the other formats is unchanged here.
