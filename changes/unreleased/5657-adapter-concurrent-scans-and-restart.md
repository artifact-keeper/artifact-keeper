section: Fixed
issues: [#5657]

- **Concurrent image scans no longer fail on trivy's cache lock, and a scan whose scanner-adapter was replaced mid-scan is resubmitted instead of failing** ([#5657]). trivy locks its on-disk layer-analysis cache for the whole of an image scan and gives up after 5s, so of six image scans fired at once against one scanner-adapter, three failed with `cache may be in use by another process: timeout`. The adapter now lets one image scan at a time use that cache and runs the others with trivy's memory cache backend (same report, no layer reuse), and reruns a scan on the memory backend when the lock is held by another process sharing the cache volume. It also runs at most `SCANNER_MAX_CONCURRENT_SCANS` scans at once (default 4, `0` for no bound); later jobs wait in a queue and report `X-Scan-Status: Queued` on their 302 report response, and the backend logs the wait and does not charge it to its poll budget. Separately, the adapter keeps jobs in memory, so after a `helm upgrade` replaced the adapter pod mid-scan the backend polled an id the new pod never had and recorded `404 unknown scan id` as a failed scan. The backend now keeps polling for up to two minutes while the adapter is unreachable or its Service answers 502/503/504, so it reaches the replacement pod, and its image and filesystem adapter clients submit the scan again when the report poll answers 404, or when the job failed on the cache lock (which also covers adapters older than 1.7.0), with a short backoff and at most three submissions in all; every other failure still fails the scan closed on the first attempt. The adapter-side change ships in scanner-adapter 1.7.0.
