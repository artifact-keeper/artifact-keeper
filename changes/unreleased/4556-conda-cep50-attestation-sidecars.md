- **Conda channels serve CEP-50 attestation sidecars and advertise them in repodata, and attestations are readable by anyone who can read the package** (#4556). A CEP-27 attestation uploaded to `PUT /conda/{repo}/{subdir}/{file}/attestation` was stored on the artifact but never served where a client looks for it, a second upload silently replaced the first, and `GET .../attestation` demanded credentials even on a public channel. Attestations are now append-only (an identical bundle is not stored twice; a different one is added beside the earlier ones), the sidecar (the JSON array of Sigstore bundles) is stored verbatim and served at `GET /conda/{repo}/{subdir}/{file}.sigs` and, immutably, at `{file}.sigs.{sha256}`, and every repodata record of an attested package carries `attestations_sha256`, so `repodata.json`, its `.bz2`/`.zst` encodings, `current_repodata.json` and the CEP-16 shards all agree. Virtual channels serve the sidecar from the member that holds the package, and remote channels proxy the upstream's. `GET .../attestation` and the sidecars follow the repository's read access: anonymous on a public channel, authenticated on a private one. `metadata.attestation` and `metadata.attestation_verification` still name the most recent upload.
