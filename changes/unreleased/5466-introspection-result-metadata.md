section: Security
issues: [#5466]

- **MCP introspection results with invalid observation or cache metadata are refused before anything changes** (#5466). A worker result could carry an unknown `cacheScope` or a negative `ttlMs` (silently dropped) and was stored anyway, and its `observedAt` was only checked inside the completion transaction, after the retry path. A result whose `observedAt` lies more than five minutes ahead of the registry clock, whose `cacheScope` is not `public` or `private`, or whose `ttlMs` is negative or above 30 days is now refused with `400 mcp_invalid_result_metadata` (with the offending `field`) for every outcome, before the job is locked: no snapshot, current-view change, audit record or job transition. The claim stays usable, so the worker can post a corrected result before its lease expires. An `observedAt` inside the five-minute window is still accepted and stored as of its arrival; the `observedAt` refusals of #5464 now use the same error code instead of `mcp_invalid_capabilities`. The `ak-mcp-introspector` worker normalises a remote server's hints to match: a `cacheScope` other than `public` is reported as `private` (`caller_specific`) and `ttlMs` is capped at 30 days, and a refused result is logged with its `field`.
